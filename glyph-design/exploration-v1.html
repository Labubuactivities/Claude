REFINERY · EDIT-THE-TEXT · ITEM 7 OF 14   · close
You wrote "every morning I bought fruit at the bazaar" — but this is a habit, not a single past event. Persian marks habitual past on the verb itself.
هر روز صبح از بازار میوه خریدم.
هر روز صبح از بازار میوه می‌خریدم.
metalinguistic · A2→B1
Close — that's the perfective stem (a single completed event). You want the imperfective: prefix می‍- attaches to the past stem for habitual, ongoing, or repeated past action.

Self-critique · 1–5

Philosophy
5
Every variant carries a point of view drawn straight from the brief — author not tourist, calm not slot-machine, expertise respected. No variant is neutral.
Hierarchy
4
Persian script gets primacy in greetings and Inkwell, metadata recedes to mono. A3 packs three columns of metadata that could read as flat if the closing ring weren't visually load-bearing.
Detail
5
Real Persian words, accurate etymology tags, FSRS opacity table tied to spec, +0.1 line-height handled in Vazirmatn-loaded blocks, coral underline only when R<0.9, mascot anchor reserved only in Refinery contexts (Fork D), not in Today (correct per brief).
Function
5
Each fork isolates one decision and gives identifiable IDs to pick by. Same content across variants so the comparison is honest. Stated goal of the artifact (let user pick) is the artifact's structure.
Innovation
4
B3's manuscript-margin Inkwell and C3's Filter-Jokers loadout slots are the genuinely novel proposals. A1–A3 are well-trodden archetypes deliberately; the point is for you to pick a posture, not for me to invent one.
